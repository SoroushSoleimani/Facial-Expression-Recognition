Facial Expression Recognition

A complete computer vision pipeline that classifies human facial expressions into 8 emotion categories, from raw pixels to an interactive Grad-CAM explainability demo — built as an AI course project at K. N. Toosi University of Technology.

02

what it detects

Trained on the FERPlus dataset, the model classifies grayscale 48×48 face crops into one of eight expressions:

Angry
Contempt
Disgust
Fear
Happy
Neutral
Sad
Surprise
03

pipeline

01

Data acquisition & cleaning

Auto-downloads FERPlus via Kaggle's kagglehub, deliberately injects noisy labels to simulate real-world data, then validates and produces a clean, trustworthy training set.

02

Preprocessing & augmentation

Resizes to 48×48 grayscale, normalizes pixel values, and augments with rotation, zoom, shifts, and horizontal flips to improve generalization.

03

Model training

Two CNN architectures trained with Adam and categorical cross-entropy, using early stopping and learning-rate reduction on plateau.

04

Evaluation

Accuracy, weighted F1-score, and confusion matrices across all 8 classes, with full visual reports in the project notebooks.

05

Interactive deployment

A Streamlit interface with live webcam and image upload, Grad-CAM heatmaps, and real-time Plotly probability charts.

04

two models, benchmarked

baseline model

  • 3 standard convolutional blocks
  • Lightweight, fast to train
  • Used to establish a performance floor

final model

  • Deeper VGG-style architecture
  • Double convolutions + batch normalization
  • L2 regularization, dropout 0.2 → 0.5
